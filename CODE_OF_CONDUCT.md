# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and maintainers of **LabGuide Studio** (`Jesse in the Lab`) pledge to make participation in our community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:
- Demonstrating empathy and kindness toward other contributors.
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback focused on Windows 11 reliability, local privacy, and documentation quality.
- Focusing on what is best for users relying on 100% local, privacy-preserving workflows.

Examples of unacceptable behavior:
- Harassment, trolling, insulting comments, or personal/political attacks.
- Publishing others' private information or unredacted screenshots/credentials without permission.
- Submitting code containing cloud telemetry, unauthorized avatar likenesses, or external data exfiltration.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove or reject commits, issues, or pull requests that violate this Code of Conduct. Issues may be reported via the repository's GitHub Issues or maintainer contact channels.
